# 📊 Global YouTube Statistics — Tableau Dashboard

An interactive Tableau workbook exploring the **Global YouTube Statistics 2023** dataset — top creators, category trends, and the relationship between subscribers, views, and earnings.

**Author:** Ebaa Nsairat

---

## 🗂️ Dashboards

### 1. Global Overview
A high-level look at the top YouTube channels worldwide, with a parameter to switch between viewing the **Top 5 / 10 / 15 / 20** creators by subscriber count.
> **Insight:** The US and India lead in top creators, showing a strong link between global reach and high annual revenue.

### 2. Categories & Trends
Breaks down channels by content category and by the year channels were created.
> **Insight:** Entertainment dominates market share, while peak channel creation (2014–2016) aligns with maximum view growth.

### 3. Correlation & Earnings
A dual-axis analysis comparing subscriber count against viewership efficiency, and how that relates to yearly earnings across categories.
> **Insight:** While some channels lead in subscribers, others achieve higher viewership efficiency — which directly influences their yearly earnings distribution across different content categories.

---

## 📁 Data

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `Global YouTube Statistics.csv` | Raw dataset — 995 channels, with rank, subscribers, video views, category, country, earnings (low/high), creation date, and country-level indicators (population, education enrollment, unemployment rate). |

The dataset and a Tableau `.hyper` extract are packaged **inside** the `.twbx` file, so the workbook is fully self-contained — no external files or connections needed.

## 🛠️ Tools

- Tableau Desktop 2019.3 or later (or [Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/) — free, no license needed)

## ▶️ How to Open

1. Download `Book2.twbx` from this repo.
2. Open it with **Tableau Desktop** or **Tableau Public Desktop**.
3. All data and images load automatically — no setup required.

*Tip: You can also publish it to [Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/) to make it viewable in a browser without requiring Tableau installed.*
